1. About the Service

Pieces of Perception is a tool that creates personalized written guides for families of neurodiverse children. You answer a detailed questionnaire about your Child, and our system uses that information — together with evidence-informed best practices — to generate written Reports (“Report” or “Reports”) tailored to your Child’s strengths, needs, sensory profile, and communication style. “Report” means any written report generated by Pieces of Perception through the Service based on information provided by you.

What this looks like for you:

  • You accept this Agreement and confirm your legal authority to provide information about your Child before you pay. Acceptance of these terms and conditions is a requirement to use Pieces of Perception’s Service.
  • You purchase one or more Reports on our website. Payment is collected at the time of purchase, before the questionnaire is completed.
  • You will receive an email with a personal access code and a link to your private dashboard.
  • You will create an account, complete a questionnaire about your Child, and receive the written Reports you purchased.
  • Your Reports are delivered to your private dashboard, and you are notified by email when they are ready.
  • You can close your account at any time, for any reason, by emailing hello@piecesofperception.com.

You will confirm that you have the legal authority to provide information about each Child and obtain a Report for that Child. Confirmation of your legal authority is a requirement to use Pieces of Perception’s Service.

Reports are prepared with human review as part of our delivery process, and delivery times may vary. We will keep you informed of the expected timeline on your dashboard and by email.

If Pieces of Perception has not delivered a purchased Report within thirty (30) days after submission of a completed questionnaire, you may cancel the undelivered Report and receive a refund of the amount paid for that Report.

If a therapist, agency, school, or other professional partner (“Provider”) of Pieces of Perception sponsors your access to Pieces of Perception, the Provider may pay for your access to the Service, but you will interact directly with Pieces of Perception, provide your information directly to Pieces of Perception, and use the Service subject to this Agreement. Your Provider’s payment for your access does not authorize Pieces of Perception to share your questionnaire responses, Reports, or other identifiable information with your Provider. You will be separately asked whether you authorize Pieces of Perception to share that information with your Provider, and Pieces of Perception will provide your questionnaire responses and Reports directly to your Provider only if you provide that authorization; if you do not authorize sharing, Pieces of Perception will provide your Reports directly to you and will not provide your questionnaire responses or Reports to your Provider. Your Provider may provide Pieces of Perception with general information about the Provider’s services, such as service type, session format, typical session length, or service setting, which Pieces of Perception may use to tailor a Report; however, Pieces of Perception does not request child-specific medical, diagnostic, or treatment information from your Provider for this purpose.
