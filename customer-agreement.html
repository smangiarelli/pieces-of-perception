A Plain-English Summary

Pieces of Perception — Family Service Agreement

Hi — before you read the longer agreement that follows, here is the short version in normal English. The full document is thorough because my attorney wanted to make sure your family is protected. This page covers the essentials of what you are agreeing to.

What is this? You purchased one or more personalized reports from Pieces of Perception — a tool that builds personalized written guides for your child. This Agreement covers your purchase, your account, and how we handle your family’s information.

What you share: Your name and email, plus a detailed questionnaire about your child — their strengths, challenges, sensory needs, communication style, school setting, and anything else you choose to write in.

What we do with it: Generate the personalized reports you purchased, and make the product better. That is it.

What we will never do: Sell your information. Share your child’s information with anyone outside Pieces of Perception without your explicit permission. Use your child’s information for advertising.

Who else touches this data: A small handful of trusted service providers help us run the service — Stripe (payment processing; we never see your full card number), Supabase (secure storage), Anthropic / Claude (writing your reports), Resend and Google (sending account emails), Vercel (hosting), and Plausible (cookie-free analytics). Each one is contractually bound to handle your data carefully.

Keeping your reports current: If you purchased the Comprehensive Plan, your purchase includes ONE updated report run within your first year — you fill out a short “What’s Changed” questionnaire and we regenerate your reports with the new information factored in. Anyone can also subscribe to our annual Report Refresh ($44/year) for one update run per subscription year.

We don’t keep your family’s information forever. Your account stays active for 12 months after your most recent purchase (or subscription renewal). About a month before that period ends, we’ll email you a reminder to download your reports. Unless you make a new purchase or have an active Report Refresh subscription, your account — and all of your family’s information, including your reports — is then permanently deleted. This is deliberate: we don’t want to hold onto children’s records any longer than we need to.

You can also leave anytime. If you want out sooner, email hello@piecesofperception.com and we will delete your identifying information within 60 days. Deleting your information also deletes the questionnaire answers we would need to update your reports later, so any unused report updates are forfeited when your data is deleted.

This is not medical advice. The reports are supportive and informational. They are not a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or a substitute for working with your child’s doctor, therapist, or school team.

Questions? Email hello@piecesofperception.com. I read every message.

— Sarah

1. About the Service

Pieces of Perception is a tool that creates personalized written guides for families of neurodiverse children. You answer a detailed questionnaire about your Child, and our system uses that information — together with evidence-informed best practices — to generate written reports tailored to your Child’s strengths, needs, sensory profile, and communication style.

What this looks like for you:

  • You purchase one or more reports on our website. Payment is collected at the time of purchase, before the questionnaire is completed.
  • You will receive an email with a personal access code and a link to your private dashboard.
  • You will create an account, accept this Agreement, complete a questionnaire about your Child, and receive the written reports you purchased.
  • Your reports are delivered to your private dashboard, and you are notified by email when they are ready.
  • You can close your account at any time, for any reason, by emailing hello@piecesofperception.com.

Reports are prepared with human review as part of our delivery process, and delivery times may vary. We will keep you informed of the expected timeline on your dashboard and by email.

6. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

We take the security of your family’s information seriously. The information you share with us is:

  • Transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Stored in a secure database with row-level access controls — meaning that even within our infrastructure, account data is segmented per family.
  • Accessible only to a limited number of authorized personnel inside Pieces of Perception, and only when necessary to operate the service or respond to a request from you.

No system is completely secure. While we follow industry-standard practices, we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access will never occur. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify you promptly and in accordance with applicable law.

Data retention — the 12-month retention period:

We deliberately limit how long we keep your family’s information. Your account remains active for twelve (12) months after your most recent purchase or Report Refresh subscription renewal (the “Retention Period”). Each new purchase, and each subscription renewal, restarts the Retention Period.

Approximately thirty (30) days before your Retention Period ends, we will email you a notice reminding you to download your reports and explaining that a new purchase or an active Report Refresh subscription will keep your account active. We will send a final reminder approximately seven (7) days before deletion. If the Retention Period ends without a new purchase or active subscription, your account and all identifying information about you and your Child — including your questionnaire responses and your generated reports — will be permanently deleted. Because your reports are delivered as downloadable PDF files, we encourage you to download and save them when they are delivered; after deletion we cannot recover them.

You do not have to wait: you may request deletion at any time, and we will delete identifying information about you and your Child within sixty (60) days of your request. In either case, we may retain certain records (such as purchase and tax records) where required to comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements, and de-identified information that cannot reasonably be linked back to you may be retained indefinitely. Deletion — scheduled or requested — also deletes the data needed to generate report updates, so any unused report update entitlements and any remaining Report Refresh subscription term are forfeited upon deletion, without refund of amounts already paid.
