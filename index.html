Why This Exists

I Built What I Desperately Needed

For years, I sat in IEP meetings, specialist offices, and family living rooms trying to explain my son. Trying to find the right words. Trying to get the people who loved him — and the professionals who were supposed to help him — to understand what life was like for him, and what he needed from them.

It felt like a constant game of whack-a-mole. He presented one way at school and another way at home. What worked on Tuesday stopped working by Thursday. Every person in his life had a different piece of the picture, but no one had the whole thing — including me. And in the gaps between all those misunderstandings, I watched him struggle. Socially. Emotionally. Academically. Until the weight of not being understood became too much for him to carry.

The turning point came when we finally had a comprehensive plan — not just a diagnosis, but a real, specific, shareable guide to who he is and how he works. Something I could hand to every person who walks through his life — grandparents, babysitters, therapists, coaches, teachers — so they could finally see the whole picture. Something that said: here is what he needs, here is what helps, here is what to do when it gets hard. That plan changed everything.

I'm an author and a narrative therapy guide for women. I know the power of getting your story down on paper — of having language for something you've been carrying silently. So when I looked around and couldn't find a tool that did this for neurodiverse families, I built one.

Pieces of Perception exists for every parent who is spinning their wheels. Who hasn't given up, but doesn't know which direction to run. Who just needs someone to hand them a map.

— Sarah Mangiarelli, Founder

"No family should have to fight this hard just to be understood. And no child should ever feel invisible in their own story."

— Sarah Mangiarelli

Add-Ons

Care Guides for Everyone
in Your Child's Life

Consistency matters. Personalized quick-reference guides so every caregiver follows the same approach — no matter their background.

Add-On

Babysitter / Respite Care Guide

Essential need-to-knows: triggers to avoid, what to do in a crisis, regulation tools that work, and clear dos and don'ts. Designed to hand to anyone watching your child — no background required.

Add-On

Extended Family / Close Friends Guide

A warm, plain-language guide for grandparents, aunts and uncles, godparents, and the close friends who feel like family — explaining your child's experience, what helps vs. what makes it worse, and how to be a truly supportive part of the team without the clinical jargon.

Add-On

Sibling Guide

An age-appropriate social-story-style guide that validates siblings' feelings — including the protective ones — explains why their brother or sister acts the way they do, and helps them feel like an important, not forgotten, part of the family team.

Add-On

Teacher / School Staff Guide

A neuro-affirming professional guide with classroom supports, communication approaches, de-escalation steps, and tips for helping classmates understand. Perfect to attach to an IEP or share at the start of a new school year.

Add-On

Service Provider / Therapy Guide

A neuro-affirming clinical-bridge document for OTs, speech-language pathologists, social-skills group facilitators, and other therapy providers. Tunes recommendations for 1-on-1 therapy and social-group settings, aligns clinical goals with home work, and gives your provider what no intake form ever asks.

Transparency

Important Disclosures

We're committed to being clear about what this platform is and how it works.

Not a Diagnostic Tool

Pieces of Perception does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical or psychological condition. Reports are informational resources generated from family-provided data. They are not a substitute for professional clinical evaluation by physicians, psychologists, occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, or other licensed providers.

Intended Use — Supplementary Resource Only

Reports are designed to be used alongside — never in place of — professional care. We encourage families to share their reports with their child's doctors, therapists, school psychologists, IEP teams, and qualified professionals as a communication and advocacy tool.

AI-Generated Content Disclosure

Reports are generated using artificial intelligence based on your questionnaire responses. While designed to produce informed, evidence-based suggestions, AI-generated content may contain errors or omissions. All suggestions should be reviewed with your child's care team before implementation. Pieces of Perception assumes no liability for outcomes resulting from application of report content.

🛡 Security & Data Privacy

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard TLS and AES-256 encryption. We do not sell, share, or broker your personal or child data to any third party. You may request permanent deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us at hello@piecesofperception.com. Our platform is designed with COPPA privacy principles to protect children's sensitive information — we collect data only from parents and guardians, never directly from children. All AI subprocessors are governed by data processing agreements that contractually prohibit the use of your data for AI model training.

Emergency Resources

If your child is in immediate danger or experiencing a medical or psychiatric emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room. For mental health crisis support, contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) or the Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741). Pieces of Perception is not a crisis or emergency service.
