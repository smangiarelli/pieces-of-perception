Refund Policy

Our commitment to your satisfaction — clear, fair, and no fine print.

Effective Date: April 25, 2026

We want you to feel confident purchasing from Pieces of Perception. If you are not satisfied with your report, please contact us — we will make it right.

1. Our Satisfaction Commitment

Pieces of Perception offers AI-generated digital reports and guides based on the information you provide through our questionnaire. Because our products are personalized digital deliverables generated and delivered immediately upon purchase, we have established the following refund policy to be fair to all customers while protecting the integrity of our service.

2. Eligibility for a Refund

You may be eligible for a full refund within 14 days of purchase under the following circumstances:

  • You experienced a technical error that prevented your report from being generated or delivered.
  • The report you received contained a material defect — meaning significant content was clearly missing, corrupted, or unrelated to your submitted questionnaire responses.
  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase due to a processing error.
  • You are dissatisfied with the quality of your report and contact us within 14 days of purchase to explain your concern. We review all dissatisfaction requests individually and, in most cases, will issue a full refund or offer a complimentary regeneration of your report.

3. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds will generally not be issued in the following situations:

  • More than 14 days have passed since the date of purchase.
  • The report was successfully generated and delivered as described, and the refund request is based solely on a change of mind after reviewing the complete report.
  • The questionnaire was completed with inaccurate, incomplete, or intentionally misleading information that affected report quality.
  • The customer has previously received a refund for the same or a substantially similar product within the past 12 months.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email us at hello@piecesofperception.com with the following information:

  • The email address used at the time of purchase.
  • The date of purchase and the product purchased.
  • A brief description of the issue or reason for your refund request.

We will acknowledge your request within 2 business days and process approved refunds within 5–10 business days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method used at the time of purchase.

5. Report Regeneration Option

In cases where a report was generated but you are dissatisfied with the content, we may offer — at our discretion and at no additional charge — a full regeneration of your report. This option is available once per purchase and requires you to re-submit or update your questionnaire responses. A regeneration request does not waive your right to request a refund if you remain unsatisfied after receiving the regenerated report.

6. Add-On Purchases

Add-on products (Care Guides, IEP Goal Recommendations) are subject to the same 14-day refund window and eligibility criteria as primary report purchases. Bundle purchases will be refunded in full if the refund request is made within the eligible window and at least one item in the bundle qualifies for a refund under Section 2 above.

7. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute with your financial institution before contacting us, we reserve the right to provide our refund policy and transaction records to the relevant payment processor as part of the dispute resolution process. We encourage you to contact us directly first — we are committed to resolving concerns quickly and fairly without the need for third-party intervention.

8. Changes to This Policy

Pieces of Perception reserves the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be effective upon posting to this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of our services after any modification constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We will not apply changes retroactively to purchases made prior to the effective date of any modification.
