How it works

Three steps to a branded report

1

Family answers

The family completes the focused questionnaire about the specific challenge they want help with.

2

You add context

A five-question provider intake — service type, format, cadence, setting, and goal — scopes the report to your engagement.

3

Report, branded

A session-ready PDF is generated with your branding, ready to use with the family and the team.

Service type

3
Session length & cadence
e.g. 45 minutes, weekly
4
Setting
clinic, home, school, telehealth, or community
5
Engagement goal
the primary aim, in your words — the report orients to this
