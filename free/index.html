A look inside

Three of the ten

What you see
What it might be asking for
What to try right now
A meltdown right after a good day at school
Capacity ran out. Holding it together all day costs something, and home is where it’s finally safe to stop holding.
Skip the questions for the first twenty minutes. Lower the light and the noise. Debrief tomorrow, not tonight.
“I don’t know” to every question
The words aren’t there yet — or the question needs more processing time than the moment allows.
Swap open questions for two choices. Or ask again later, in writing, side by side instead of face to face.
A huge reaction to a small thing
It was the last thing, not the only thing. The stack was already full before you got there.
Name the stack, not the trigger: “That’s a lot at once.” Don’t argue about the size of the reaction.
