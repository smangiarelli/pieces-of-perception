Important Disclosures

Not a Diagnostic Tool

Pieces of Perception does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical or psychological condition. Reports are informational resources generated from family-provided data. They are not a substitute for professional clinical evaluation by physicians, psychologists, occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, or other licensed providers.

Intended Use — Supplementary Resource Only

Reports are designed to be used alongside — never in place of — professional care. We encourage families to share their reports with their child’s doctors, therapists, school psychologists, IEP teams, and qualified professionals as a communication and advocacy tool.

AI-Generated Content Disclosure

Reports are generated using artificial intelligence based on your questionnaire responses. While designed to produce informed, evidence-based suggestions, AI-generated content may contain errors or omissions. All suggestions should be reviewed with your child’s care team before implementation. Pieces of Perception assumes no liability for outcomes resulting from application of report content.

🛡 Security & Data Privacy

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard TLS and AES-256 encryption. We do not sell, share, or broker your personal or child data to any third party. You may request permanent deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us at hello@piecesofperception.com. Our platform is designed with COPPA privacy principles to protect children’s sensitive information — we collect data only from parents and guardians, never directly from children. All AI subprocessors are governed by data processing agreements that contractually prohibit the use of your data for AI model training.

Emergency Resources
