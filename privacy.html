Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Effective Date: September 9, 2026 · Version v2.0

Your family’s information is sacred to us. We collect only what we need, we never sell your data, and we treat your child’s information with the same care we would want for our own.

This Privacy Policy and our Terms and Conditions are one set of documents. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and how long we keep it. The Terms and Conditions carry the contractual terms — what you and we each agree to do. This policy is incorporated into the Terms by reference. In the event of any conflict, the Terms and Conditions control. If you are a beta family, your Beta Family Participation Agreement governs your participation and controls instead.

1. Who We Are

Pieces of Perception (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is operated by Pieces of Perception LLC, a Rhode Island limited liability company dedicated to helping families of neurodiverse children create personalized reports and care guides. Our website is piecesofperception.com.

Questions about this policy: hello@piecesofperception.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following kinds of information.

a) Information about you (the parent or guardian)

  • Your name and email address.
  • The access code issued with your purchase.
  • Your account login credentials (your password is stored only in encrypted form by our authentication provider).
  • Purchase and billing information: the items you purchased, amounts paid, and transaction identifiers. Payments are processed by Stripe, our payment processor; we do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Your acceptance record: the version of the Terms you accepted, the acknowledgments you checked, and the date and time of your acceptance.
  • Your age- and identity-verification result: the outcome of the check confirming that you are an adult, and the date it was completed. We do not receive or store the identification document itself.
  • Optional: your phone number, mailing address, or other contact information you choose to provide.

b) Information about your Child

The questionnaire is detailed and may include sensitive information. We collect only the information you choose to share with us. Examples include:

  • Your Child’s first name, age, and grade level.
  • Diagnoses or developmental information you have shared (for example, autism, ADHD, sensory processing differences).
  • Information about your Child’s communication style, sensory needs, regulation patterns, school setting, and home life.
  • Information about your household and, if you choose, about siblings or other caregivers.
  • Open-ended responses you write describing what works, what is hard, and what you wish others understood about your Child.
  • If you use a Report update or the Report Refresh subscription: your answers to the follow-up “What’s Changed” questionnaire, which are stored alongside — and do not replace — your original questionnaire responses.

We do not collect documents or records. We do not require — and we do not accept — medical records, IEP documents, Section 504 plans, evaluations, assessments, therapy records, school communications, behavioral plans, diagnostic reports, or any similar document or record about your Child. Everything we need to prepare your Reports comes from the questionnaire itself. The Service has no feature for uploading or attaching documents. If you send us such a document anyway, we will not use it to prepare your Reports, we will not add it to your account, and we will delete it. We will let you know that we have done so. The corresponding restriction on you is in Section 8 of the Terms and Conditions.

c) Technical information

  • Basic, privacy-respecting analytics from Plausible (no cookies, no cross-site tracking, and no storage of full IP addresses by Plausible itself).
  • Standard web server, hosting, and security logs maintained by our hosting and infrastructure providers. These logs may include technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, device information, operating system information, timestamps, referral URLs, and page-request activity for purposes including security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, and operation of the Service.
  • Error and diagnostic logs that may incidentally include account identifiers when something goes wrong.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share with us to:

  • Process your purchase and deliver the personalized written Reports you have purchased.
  • Provide Report updates and Report Refresh re-runs you are entitled to, which requires combining your original questionnaire responses with your “What’s Changed” responses.
  • Maintain your account, send you account-related and purchase-related emails, and respond when you contact us.
  • Send you occasional updates and resources if you signed up for our email list. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • Improve the Service using de-identified information — identifying bugs, refining the questionnaire, improving the quality of Reports, and testing our own internal evaluation tools. We do this only with information from which names, direct identifiers, and specific identifying details have been removed.
  • Conduct or publish research, and use de-identified information in marketing or other public-facing materials — only if you have opted in to the De-Identified Information release described in Section 21 of the Terms and Conditions. You are never required to agree to this, and you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Comply with our legal obligations and protect the safety and rights of families, our company, and the public.

We will never sell your information or your Child’s information to a third party. We will not use your Child’s information for advertising. We will not share identifiable information about your family with anyone outside of Pieces of Perception without your explicit consent, except as described in Section 4 below or as required by law.

If you separately authorize us to share your information with a therapist, agency, school, or other professional partner that sponsors your access to Pieces of Perception, we may share the information covered by your authorization with that professional partner as described in Section 1 of the Terms and Conditions.

4. How We Share Your Information

To operate Pieces of Perception, we rely on a small number of trusted service providers. Each one is named below, with what they handle and a link to their own privacy and security practices.

  • Stripe. Processes payments and subscriptions. Stripe receives your payment card details directly; we do not store your full card number. Where we use Stripe Identity to confirm that an account holder is an adult, Stripe performs that check and returns only the result to us — we never receive or store the identification document itself. (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Supabase. Stores your account information, questionnaire responses, and Reports. Data is hosted in the United States. (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Anthropic (Claude). Generates the written portions of your personalized Reports. We send the relevant questionnaire content to the Claude API; per Anthropic’s commercial terms, this content is not used to train Claude’s models. (anthropic.com/legal)
  • Resend. Sends you transactional emails (account confirmations, Report-ready notices). (resend.com/legal/privacy-policy)
  • Google (Apps Script). Runs our email-notification automation. When a submission or delivery event occurs, the automation receives the associated account and questionnaire record — which includes your name and email address, your Child’s first name and age, and the items you purchased — for the purpose of sending notifications. (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Vercel. Hosts the Pieces of Perception website. (vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)
  • Plausible Analytics. Provides cookie-free, privacy-friendly visit metrics. (plausible.io/privacy)
  • GoDaddy. Manages our domain name registrations. (No personal information about you is stored here.)

Each of these providers handles our data in accordance with their own privacy and security commitments. We require service providers that process personal information on our behalf to use that information only as necessary to provide their services to Pieces of Perception and subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements. The providers we use may change over time; the list in this Section is always the current one, and the effective date at the top of this policy tells you when it was last updated.

To generate Reports and other outputs requested by you, we may transmit relevant questionnaire responses and other information you provide to third-party artificial intelligence service providers acting on our behalf. We use commercial-tier services that, pursuant to the provider’s applicable terms, do not use customer-submitted content to train the provider’s general artificial intelligence models. We use your information for the purpose of generating the Reports and outputs you request. Except as expressly authorized through any optional consent you separately provide, we will not use your identifiable information to train internal artificial intelligence models or for broader research or product-development purposes beyond operating the Service for your account.

We may also access, preserve, and disclose information as required or permitted by applicable law, including to comply with a subpoena, court order, warrant, governmental request, or other applicable legal process; to report suspected child abuse, neglect, or exploitation; to address a serious threat to the safety of a Child or another person; or to otherwise comply with our legal obligations. See Section 23 (Miscellaneous) and Section 26 (Sensitive Disclosures) of the Terms and Conditions.

Before we collect any information about your Child, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old, that you are the parent, legal guardian, or other adult with the legal authority to share information about your Child, that you consent on your Child’s behalf to our collection and use of that information, and that everything you have told us about your authority is truthful and accurate.

The full parental-consent terms, including how we verify that the person providing information is an adult with the authority to provide it, are set out in Section 5 of the Terms and Conditions, which controls. We keep those terms in one place so that there is never a question about which version applies.

You may at any time request access to the information we have collected concerning your Child, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion (subject to applicable legal retention requirements), or withdraw your consent on a going-forward basis, by contacting us at hello@piecesofperception.com. We may take reasonable steps to verify the identity and authority of the requesting individual before responding.

You should not rely on Pieces of Perception to identify, investigate, or respond to such concerns. However, if Pieces of Perception becomes aware of information concerning suspected child abuse, neglect, exploitation, or a serious threat to the safety of a Child or another person, we may disclose relevant information to law enforcement, child protective services, emergency services, or other appropriate authorities when required or permitted by applicable law. See Section 26 of the Terms and Conditions.

6. Data Storage and Security

The information you share with us is:

  • Transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Stored in a secure database with row-level access controls — meaning that even within our infrastructure, account data is segmented per family. Row-level access controls are designed to prevent one family's account from accessing another family's account information.
  • Accessible only to a limited number of authorized personnel inside Pieces of Perception, and only when necessary to operate the Service or respond to a request from you.

Pieces of Perception is currently a one-person company. Sarah, our founder, holds the administrative access needed to operate the Service — to generate your reports, correct problems, and respond to your requests — and she is the only person who has it. Should Pieces of Perception need to grow, administrative access is restricted to authorized personnel and contractors who require access to operate, maintain, secure, or support the Service.

No system is completely secure. While we follow industry-standard practices, we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access will never occur. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify you promptly and in accordance with applicable law. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

We delete your family’s information on purpose, on a schedule. We do not want to hold children’s records any longer than we need to, so retention is built in rather than left to us to remember.

  • Unless you have an active Report Refresh subscription, your account remains active for twelve (12) months after your most recent purchase (the “Retention Period”). Each new purchase restarts it.
  • With an active Report Refresh subscription, your account stays active throughout the subscription period, and a new twelve (12)-month Retention Period begins when your last paid subscription period ends.
  • If you use a Report update, a new twelve (12)-month Retention Period for that Report begins when the updated Report is delivered.
  • Approximately thirty (30) days before the Retention Period ends we email you a reminder to download your Reports, and a final reminder approximately seven (7) days before deletion.
  • If the Retention Period ends without a new purchase or active subscription, your account and all identifying information about you and your Child — questionnaire responses and generated Reports included — is permanently deleted. Your Reports are downloadable PDFs; please save them when they arrive, because after deletion we cannot recover them.

You do not have to wait. You may request deletion at any time, and we will delete identifying information about you and your Child within forty-five (45) days of your request. In either case we may retain certain records (such as purchase and tax records) where required to comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements, and de-identified information that cannot reasonably be linked back to you may be retained indefinitely.

What deletion costs you. Deletion — scheduled or requested — also removes the data we would need to generate a Report update, so any unused update or remaining subscription benefit ends with it, without refund. Those consequences are contractual and are set out in Section 6 of the Terms and Conditions, which controls.

9. Cookies and Browser Storage

We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking, and our analytics provider (Plausible) is cookie-free. What we do use is your browser’s own local storage, on your device, for two purposes:

  • Keeping you signed in. When you sign in, your session is stored in your browser so you are not asked to sign in again on every page. Signing out removes it.
  • Saving your questionnaire as you go. So that you can step away and come back, your in-progress answers are saved in your browser on that device. This draft never leaves your device until you press submit, and it is deleted from your browser as soon as you do. If you start a questionnaire on a shared or public computer and do not finish it, the draft stays in that browser — you can clear it by clearing that browser’s site data for piecesofperception.com.

You can disable or clear browser storage in your browser settings, though signing in and saving a questionnaire in progress will not work without it.
