Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Your family's information is sacred to us. We collect only what we need, we never sell your data, and we treat your child's information with the same care we would want for our own.

If you have purchased from us, the Family Service Agreement you accepted at checkout also describes how we handle your information — in more detail, and in binding terms. In the event of any conflict between this policy and that Agreement, the Agreement controls.

1. Who We Are

Pieces of Perception ("we," "our," or "us") is operated by Pieces of Perception LLC, a company dedicated to helping families of neurodiverse children create personalized action reports and care guides. Our website is located at piecesofperception.com.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at: hello@piecesofperception.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically when you use our site.

Information you provide:

  • Waitlist email address — If you join our waitlist, we collect your email address so we can notify you when we launch.
  • Questionnaire responses — When you use our platform to create a report, you will provide information about your child's needs, strengths, sensory profile, and communication style. This information is used solely to generate your personalized report.
  • Account information — When you create an account, we collect your name and email address.
  • Payment information — When you make a purchase, payment is processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your credit card number.

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic usage data such as pages visited and time spent on the site (via standard web analytics)
  • Your browser type and device type
  • IP address (used for security and fraud prevention only)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you launch updates and product announcements if you joined our waitlist (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Generate your personalized action reports and care guides
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our platform
  • Process payments and send receipts
  • Respond to your questions and provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes — ever.

We work with a small number of trusted service providers who help us operate the platform. This list matches Section 4 of the Family Service Agreement:

  • Stripe — payment and subscription processing. Stripe receives your card details directly; we never store your full card number.
  • Supabase — secure database and authentication. Stores your account information, questionnaire responses, and reports, hosted in the United States.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — generates the written portions of your reports. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this content is not used to train their models.
  • Resend — sends transactional email (account confirmations, report-ready notices).
  • Google (Apps Script) — runs our email-notification automation. When a submission or delivery event occurs, the automation receives the associated account and questionnaire record — which includes your name and email address, your child's first name and age, and the items you purchased — for the purpose of sending notifications.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Plausible Analytics — cookie-free, privacy-friendly visit metrics.
  • GoDaddy — domain registration. No personal information about you is stored here.

We require service providers that process personal information on our behalf to use it only as necessary to provide their services to us, subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements. The providers we use may change over time; the current list is maintained here and in the Family Service Agreement. We may also share information when required by law or to protect the safety of our users — see Section 23 of the Agreement for how we handle subpoenas and other legal process.

5. Children's Privacy

Our platform is designed to be used by parents and caregivers on behalf of their children — not directly by children themselves. By completing the questionnaire, you represent that you are the parent or legal guardian of the child whose information you are providing, or that you otherwise have authorization to share that information.

The questionnaire collects identifying information such as a child's first name and age, as well as sensitive developmental, behavioral, and health information (including sensory profiles, communication patterns, trauma history, and medication use). This information is used solely to generate the personalized report you have requested and is not shared with third parties except as described in Section 4.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

How we obtain that consent, in order: before we collect anything about your child, you (1) review and accept the Family Service Agreement, (2) separately confirm that you are over 18 and are the parent, legal guardian, or other person legally authorized to provide information about that child, and (3) complete a credit or debit card transaction, which is also used to verify parental consent. Only after all three steps does the questionnaire open. We record the version of the Agreement you accepted, which boxes you checked, and the date and time.

If you believe your child has provided information to our platform without your consent, please contact us immediately at hello@piecesofperception.com and we will delete it promptly.

Sensitive disclosures: If you share information in the questionnaire suggesting that a child is currently being abused, neglected, or is in danger, please know that Pieces of Perception is not a mandated reporter and cannot take action on disclosures made through our platform. If a child is in immediate danger, please call 911 or contact your local child protective services directly.

5a. A Note on HIPAA

Pieces of Perception is not a "covered entity" under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) because we are a consumer informational tool, not a healthcare provider, health plan, or healthcare clearinghouse. Accordingly, HIPAA's specific regulatory requirements do not apply to our platform.

However, because the information you share with us is sensitive in nature, we voluntarily follow privacy practices consistent with HIPAA's underlying principles: we collect only what is necessary, store it securely, do not sell it, and give you the ability to access, correct, and delete it. We treat your family's information with the same care we would want for our own.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's secure infrastructure, which uses industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. We use Row Level Security (RLS) policies so that when you are signed in you can only ever reach your own family's records — one family's account can never see another's.

Pieces of Perception is a one-person company. Sarah, our founder, holds the administrative access needed to operate the Service — to generate your reports, correct problems, and respond to your requests — and she is the only person who has it. She uses that access only when it is necessary to run the Service or to answer a request from you.

Questionnaire responses sent to our AI report generation provider (Anthropic) are transmitted under a data processing agreement that prohibits the use of your data for AI model training. Your child's information is used only to generate your report and is not retained by Anthropic beyond that purpose.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe from waitlist or marketing emails at any time using the link in any email we send
  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information in your account
  • Delete your account and associated data by contacting us at hello@piecesofperception.com
  • Opt out of analytics tracking using your browser's privacy settings or a browser extension

If you are located in California, the European Union, or another jurisdiction with specific privacy rights, additional rights may apply. Please contact us to learn more.

8. Cookies

We use minimal, essential cookies to keep you logged in and to make our platform function properly. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some features of the platform.

9. Retention

We delete your family's information on purpose, on a schedule. We do not want to hold children's records any longer than we need to, so retention is built in rather than left to us to remember.

  • Unless you have an active Report Refresh subscription, your account stays active for twelve (12) months after your most recent purchase (the “Retention Period”). Each new purchase restarts it.
  • With an active Report Refresh subscription, your account stays active throughout the subscription period, and a new 12-month Retention Period begins when your last paid subscription period ends.
  • If you use a report update, a new 12-month Retention Period for that report begins when the updated report is delivered.
  • About 30 days before the Retention Period ends we email you a reminder to download your reports, and a final reminder about 7 days before deletion.
  • If the Retention Period ends without a new purchase or active subscription, your account and all identifying information about you and your child — questionnaire responses and generated reports included — is permanently deleted. Your reports are downloadable PDFs; please save them when they arrive, because after deletion we cannot recover them.

You do not have to wait. You may request deletion at any time, and we will delete identifying information about you and your child within sixty (60) days of your request. In either case we may retain certain records (such as purchase and tax records) where required to comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements, and de-identified information that cannot reasonably be linked back to you may be retained indefinitely.

Deletion — scheduled or requested — also removes the data we would need to generate a report update, so any unused update or remaining subscription benefit ends with it, without refund. The controlling version of these terms is Section 6 of the Family Service Agreement.
