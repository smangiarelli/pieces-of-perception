This Agreement explains what the beta program is, what information you and your family will share with us, how we will use and protect that information, and the legal terms that apply to your participation. Throughout this Agreement, “we,” “us,” and “Pieces of Perception” refer to Pieces of Perception LLC, a Rhode Island limited liability company. “You” refers to the parent or legal guardian who signs this Agreement on behalf of yourself and your minor child or children (referred to here as “your Child”).

1. About the Beta Program

Pieces of Perception is a tool that creates personalized written guides for families of neurodiverse children. You answer a detailed questionnaire about your Child, and our system uses that information — together with evidence-informed best practices — to generate a written report tailored to your Child's strengths, needs, sensory profile, and communication style.

The beta program is a limited, invitation-only early version of this product. By participating, you are helping us improve the experience before we open it to the public. The beta is provided to you free of charge.

What this looks like for you:

  • You will receive a personal invitation code and a private link to the beta site.
  • You will create an account, complete a questionnaire about your Child, and receive one or more written reports.
  • You may be asked, from time to time, to share feedback about your experience.
  • You can withdraw at any time, for any reason, by emailing hello@piecesofperception.com.

2. Information We Collect

During the beta, we collect the following kinds of information:

a) Information about you (the parent or guardian)

  • Your name and email address.

  • Optional: your phone number, mailing address, or other contact information you choose to provide.

  • Your Child's first name, age, and grade level.

  • Information about your household and, if you choose, about siblings or other caregivers.
  • Open-ended responses you write describing what works, what is hard, and what you wish others understood about your Child.

Each of these providers is contractually bound to handle our data in accordance with their own privacy and security commitments. We do not send your Child's information to any service provider not listed above.

6. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

We take the security of your family's information seriously. The information you share with us is:

  • Transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Stored in a secure database with row-level access controls — meaning that even within our infrastructure, account data is segmented per family.
  • Accessible only to a limited number of authorized personnel inside Pieces of Perception, and only when necessary to operate the service or respond to a request from you.

No system is completely secure. While we follow industry-standard practices, we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access will never occur. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify you promptly and in accordance with applicable law.

Data retention:

We retain your account information and questionnaire responses for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to operate, support, and improve the service. If you withdraw from the beta or close your account, we will delete identifying information about you and your Child within sixty (60) days, except where we are required to retain certain records to comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. De-identified information that cannot reasonably be linked back to you may be retained indefinitely.
